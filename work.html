Admach Systems Limited — Successful Listing

Naavick Legal acted as the Sole Legal Counsel for the proposed Initial Public Offering of Admach Systems Limited, with In-Principal Approval now received — a milestone that reflects both the strength of the transaction and the rigour of the legal work behind it.

Admach Systems Limited is an established name in Special Purpose Machines in India, headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra. The firm brings decades of manufacturing expertise to a specialised segment of Indian industry, and this listing marks a significant step in its growth journey.

The transaction was led by a dedicated team at Naavick Legal comprising Bhupendra Dave, Shritee Ghosh, Anuradha Sangeeta Parmar, Muskan Agrawal, and Hitesh Chhangani. Congratulations to the Admach Systems team on a successful listing — and to the Naavick team on a mandate executed with precision.

Rappid Valves (India) Limited — Successful Listing on NSE Emerge

Naavick Legal's Capital Markets team is proud to have been part of the successful listing of Rappid Valves (India) Limited on NSE Emerge — a mandate that underscores the firm's growing presence in the SME capital markets space.

Well done, team Naavick.

Cian Healthcare Limited — Resolution Plan Approved by NCLT Mumbai

Naavick Legal acted as the Legal Counsel before the National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench VI, for the Successful Resolution Applicant in the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process of Cian Healthcare Limited — a listed pharmaceutical company based in Pune, Maharashtra.

The Resolution Plan was submitted by Mr. Pradeep Kumar Jain, Founder and Director of Ananta Medicare Limited, a prominent pharmaceutical group established in 1999 with manufacturing plants in Sri Ganganagar, Rajasthan. Ananta Medicare holds EU GMP, WHO GMP, and multiple international certifications, and exports to markets including Ukraine, Russia, the Philippines, and the United States. The Resolution Plan was approved unanimously by the Committee of Creditors with 100% voting share, and subsequently approved by the Hon'ble Tribunal vide its order pronounced on 18 December 2025 under Section 31(1) of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.

The matter before the NCLT involved a complex CIRP spanning multiple restoration, reconstitution, and extension proceedings, with creditors including IDBI Bank, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India, and SIDBI. Naavick Legal, represented by Adv. Bhupendra Dave, acted as counsel on record for the Successful Resolution Applicant throughout the proceedings — from plan submission to final approval — ensuring the resolution was executed with rigour and in full compliance with the Code.
