Insolvency & Restructuring

We advise creditors, corporate debtors, resolution professionals, investors, and other stakeholders across the full spectrum of insolvency and restructuring matters. Combining deep legal expertise with commercial acumen, we deliver strategic, practical, and outcome-oriented solutions under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, ensuring seamless representation through every stage of the insolvency process.

Scope of work
Initiation and representation in insolvency proceedings under Sections 7, 9, and 10 of the IBC
Strategic advisory and comprehensive legal support to Resolution Professionals
Advisory on avoidance transactions
Legal review, due diligence, and advisory and drafting of resolution plans
Assistance with post-approval implementation and regulatory compliances of the Resolution Plan
End-to-end advisory and representation in personal insolvency and bankruptcy matters
Comprehensive legal support in voluntary and compulsory liquidation proceedings
