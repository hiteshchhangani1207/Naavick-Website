IBC Amendment Act, 2026
Clause-by-Clause Analysis

The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (Amendment) Act, 2026 — Act No. 6 of 2026, assented 6 April 2026 — is the most significant reform to India's insolvency framework since 2016.

Act No. 6 of 2026  ·  72 Clauses  ·  Analysis by Naavick Legal  ·  June 2026

  • Vidarbha overruled — mandatory admission
  • Rainbow Papers reversed — waterfall reform
  • Clean slate codified retrospectively
  • CLRP — new creditor-led resolution track
  • Group Insolvency framework (Ch. VA)
  • Dissenting FC floor settled (Amit Metallics)
  • Liquidation hard-timelined — 180 + 90 days
  • ss. 74 & 76 decriminalised
