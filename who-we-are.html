The Firm

Not merely a firm —
a trust, earned.

Founded in 2021 with offices across Mumbai, Delhi, and Nagpur, Naavick Legal brings together a multidisciplinary team of advocates and company secretaries united by one conviction — that exceptional legal work begins with exceptional research.

We practice across Corporate Law, Securities Laws, Insolvency & Restructuring under the IBC, Capital Markets, Foreign Investments, and the Startup ecosystem. Our work spans advisory, documentation, compliance, and litigation — anchored in the same rigour regardless of mandate size.

In four years, Naavick has become trusted counsel to listed companies, promoter groups, resolution professionals, foreign investors, and first-generation founders. Not because we market broadly — but because we execute precisely.

Research Before Everything

We begin every engagement with a question, not an assumption. Our counsel is grounded in deep research of regulation, precedent, and risk — before a single piece of advice leaves the room.

Clients work directly with principals. There are no layers of associates, no diluted attention. Every mandate receives the full focus of the person responsible for its outcome.
