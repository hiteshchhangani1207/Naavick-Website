About Naavick.

With over 12 years of expertise in Corporate and Securities Laws, including extensive work in IBC, Capital Market, and Start-up ecosystem for the past 7 years, Bhupendra Dave, an Advocate and qualified Company Secretary, established Naavick Legal in 2021. Nestled in Mumbai, Naavick Legal is a research-driven legal advisory and litigation firm with a mission to possess authoritative knowledge in corporate laws and practical command over execution.

The vision of our firm is to evolve into a research-driven legal think tank capable of forecasting and resolving policy and regulatory issues in corporate laws. Our team comprises highly motivated and technically skilled lawyers, united by a shared dedication to surpassing expectations in the delivery of legal services. With a keen focus on emerging legal trends, our firm brings a collective commitment to precision and thoroughness, ensuring exceptional legal solutions.

From the Founder.

At Naavick, our journey has been guided by a simple belief—exceptional legal counsel is built on trust, integrity, and a deep understanding of our clients' business realities. From the outset, our vision has been to combine the timeless values of hard work, dedication, and professionalism with innovative, practical solutions that help clients navigate an increasingly complex legal landscape.

We are committed to delivering client-centric legal services that are not only legally sound but also commercially relevant. Our approach is rooted in understanding the unique challenges faced by businesses and providing strategic advice that creates lasting value.

Equally important is the culture we have built within our firm. We foster an environment of inclusivity, collaboration, and continuous learning, where diverse perspectives are valued and intellectual curiosity is encouraged. By embracing technology and progressive work practices, we empower our team to remain agile, efficient, and forward-thinking.

As we continue to grow, our commitment remains unchanged—to be trusted advisors who deliver excellence with integrity, pursue innovation with purpose, and build enduring relationships with every client we serve.

Adv. Bhupendra Dave · Founding Partner
